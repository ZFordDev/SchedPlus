# Privacy Policy

Your schedule is your business, not ours.

SchedPlus is built around a simple idea: **your data belongs to you.**

## What stays on your device?

SchedPlus stores your tasks, notes, reminders, preferences, and task history locally.

- No accounts required.
- No analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising or tracking.
- No automatic cloud synchronisation.
- No uploading your tasks or personal information.

You can back up, export, and restore your data whenever you like.

## What about internet access?

SchedPlus can check for application updates using GitHub.

Depending on how you installed it, updates may instead be handled by the Snap Store or Microsoft Store.

**Your tasks are not included in update checks.**

You can disable automatic update checks in **Settings -> General**.

## Where is my data?

Your tasks are stored in a local database on your device.

You can find its exact location in **Settings -> Data**.

Backups and exports are saved wherever you choose. SchedPlus doesn't upload them.

## Questions?

Have a privacy concern or spotted something that doesn't look right?

Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/ZFordDev/SchedPlus/issues), or take a look at the source code yourself.
