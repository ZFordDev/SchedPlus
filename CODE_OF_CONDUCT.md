# Code of Conduct

We're here to build useful software, not argue with strangers on the internet.

## The basics

- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions constructive and on topic.
- Remember that everyone has different skills and experience.

Disagreements are fine. Being unpleasant about them isn't.

## Keeping things friendly

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or reject contributions when necessary.

If you experience or witness inappropriate behaviour, please contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.

## That's it!

SchedPlus is an open-source project, and contributions of all sizes are welcome.
