## Product name

SchedPlus

## Short description

Plan tasks and time locally, without an account.

## Description

SchedPlus is a modern, local-first scheduler for planning tasks and time. Add
tasks, organise your day, and view scheduled work in a clear desktop calendar.
Your task data stays on your device in your Windows user profile; SchedPlus does
not require an account or a network connection for its core features.
