## Name

SchedPlus

## Summary

Plan tasks and time with a local-first scheduler.

## Description

SchedPlus Standard is a modern, local-first scheduler for planning tasks and
time without an account or network connection. Add and organise tasks in a
focused PyQt desktop workspace, then use native month, week, and day calendar
views to understand your schedule.

Task data is stored locally in your Snap user data and is preserved across
automatic refreshes. SchedPlus does not require an account or network access
for its core features.
